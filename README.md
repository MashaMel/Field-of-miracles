Field of miracles.
This is a game like a tv show "Field of miracles"
At first yju will need to enter a letter. Than you will have to enter a letter or a word with length, that equals numeber of dashes on the screen. Game will tell you if you're right or not and it will offer you a clue.
Your task is to guess the word. There are 3 rounds, with 10 attempts in each round. In the first attempt, you enter a letter, from the second to the ninth - either a word or a letter, and in the tenth - only a word. After 1-9 attempts, you will be offered a hint. If you want a hint, enter 1, if not - 0 (if you ask for a hint several times, it will be the same)
